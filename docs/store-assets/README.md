# Prepared Google Play screenshot cards

Three 1080 × 1920 RGB PNG cards use full, unmodified app captures from an owner-supplied earlier recording, placed in Blender-rendered phone frames. Captions and decoration sit outside the app screen. Original capture resolution is 576 × 1296; the cards do not claim a higher-resolution source capture or captures from version 1.0.14.

| File | Demonstrated behavior | Source time |
|---|---|---|
| [03-run-in-background.png](03-run-in-background.png) | Start in the app, then choose Run in background | 00:15 |
| [04-floating-controls.png](04-floating-controls.png) | Tap the Open NoBonk pill to return to controls | 00:18 |
| [05-stay-aware.png](05-stay-aware.png) | Acknowledge the Stay aware reminder | 00:03 |

The depicted high-level controls and reminder were checked against Android 1.0.14 (`21953b7`). Styling comes from the earlier recording. The older 00:42 floating Stop panel is deliberately excluded; version 1.0.14 offers Stop in the app and notification. No expanded sound/voice settings screen was invented. Visible metre presets are approximate sensitivity labels, not verified distance boundaries.

[manifest.json](manifest.json) records the source recording hash, frame times, captions and final PNG hashes without private local paths. These are prepared store assets, not proof that Google Play has approved the listing.
